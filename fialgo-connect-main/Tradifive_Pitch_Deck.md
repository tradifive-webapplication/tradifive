## 1. The Problem
**Retail Traders Are Set Up to Fail**
Over 90% of retail traders lose money. The root cause is not a lack of strategy, but poor emotional control and fragmented tooling.

1. **Lack of Discipline**: Revenge trading and blown accounts are the primary killers of trader longevity. Existing platforms do not intervene to protect the user.
2. **Fragmented Workflow**: Traders execute on their broker's app (MT5), journal in messy Excel spreadsheets, and seek community in chaotic Discord or Telegram groups.
3. **Isolation**: Trading is a lonely profession without a structured, dedicated community to share setups, mentor, and grow safely.

<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>

## 2. The Solution
**An All-In-One Ecosystem that Protects and Empowers**
Tradifive unifies automated risk management, smart journaling, and a professional social network into a single, seamless platform.

- **RiskGuard (Live Risk Management)**: Real-time monitoring acts as an emergency kill-switch and drawdown protector to prevent blown accounts.
- **Automated Journaling & AI Coach**: Syncs trades automatically, calculates a proprietary "Discipline Score", and provides actionable AI insights.
- **Connect (Social Network)**: A dedicated space for traders with feeds, professional channels, direct messaging, and verified mentor access.

<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>

## 3. Why Now?
**The Retail Trading Boom Needs Maturation**
- **Post-Pandemic Surge**: Retail trading volume has exploded globally, but the churn rate is unsustainably high because new traders lack risk management.
- **API Accessibility**: Brokers are increasingly opening up their APIs (e.g., AngelOne, MT5 bridges), allowing third-party tools to interact directly with execution layers.
- **Demand for Transparency**: The market is tired of "fake gurus." Tradifive's verified performance tracking brings much-needed transparency to trading communities.

<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>

## 5. The Product
**Built for Retention & Daily Active Use**

1. **The Dashboard**: Live tracking of P&L, Win Rate, and Discipline Score.
2. **Risk Monitor**: Users set their daily drawdown limit (e.g., 5%). If breached, Tradifive's Kill-Switch intervenes.
3. **The Journal**: Beautiful, automated visual charts of trade performance.
4. **The Feed**: A Twitter-like feed but exclusively for verified trade setups and market analysis.

## 6. Business Model
**Highly Scalable, Diversified Revenue Streams**

1. **Broker Affiliate & Rebates (Primary Engine)**
   - **How it works**: Users sign up for partner brokers through Tradifive to unlock "Tradifive Pro" for FREE.
   - **Revenue**: Tradifive earns an ongoing rebate on every trade executed.
   - **Why it wins**: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is near zero because the product is "free," but the Lifetime Value (LTV) generates continuous affiliate revenue for years.
2. **SaaS Subscriptions**
   - $19/mo for Pro features (for users who keep their existing brokers).
3. **Community Marketplace**
   - 10-15% platform fee on subscriptions for premium mentor channels.

<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>

## 7. Unit Economics & Go-To-Market (GTM)
**Viral Growth Meets High LTV**

- **GTM Strategy**: Partner with mid-tier trading influencers (YouTube/Twitter). They bring their audience to Tradifive to use the Social/Channel features. Their followers sign up for broker accounts through our affiliate links to unlock Pro tools.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: ~$5 (Acquired via influencer revenue sharing and organic virality).
- **LTV (Lifetime Value)**: ~$540 (Assuming $15/mo in broker rebates over an average 36-month trader lifespan).
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: > 10:1
