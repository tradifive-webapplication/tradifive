## 💥 The Problem

Retail trading is **lonely, emotional, and lacks transparency**. 
Traders struggle with:

1. **Isolation:** Trading alone leads to poor emotional control and "revenge trading".
2. **Fake Gurus:** Social media is flooded with fake screenshots; there is no verifiable performance tracking.
3. **Lack of Discipline:** No automated accountability or rewards for staying disciplined.

---

## 🚀 The Solution: Tradifive

Tradifive combines the high-engagement loops of Instagram with professional-grade trading utility.

- **Verifiable Community:** Real-time social feed with 24-hour stories, likes, and follows.
- **Live Market Data:** Integrated live Forex and Crypto pricing widgets directly in the feed.
- **Accountability Leaderboards:** Automated tracking of top disciplined traders and winning streaks.
- **Trading Competitions:** A gamified ecosystem where traders compete for real rewards.

## 📈 Business Model

We monetize through a 3-tier SaaS subscription model directly targeting serious retail traders:

1. **Starter (Free):** Basic social feed, delayed data, standard profile.
2. **Pro ($99/mo):** Advanced analytics, verified badges, live data access, entry to pro competitions.
3. **Elite ($499/mo):** Mastermind access, proprietary EA tools, algorithmic risk engine.
