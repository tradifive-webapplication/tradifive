Frequently Asked Questions
How do I reset my Master Kill Switch?
You can toggle the Master Kill Switch directly from your Risk Guard settings once the daily cooldown period expires.
Where are my recent trading logs?
All execution activity is tracked inside the Journal tab, which syncs seamlessly with your connected accounts.
How do I link a new broker?
Head to Settings > Linked Brokers to scan the QR code or input your MT5 server details.
