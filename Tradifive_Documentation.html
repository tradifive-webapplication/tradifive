Section 01

Platform Overview

What is Tradifive?

Tradifive is a next-generation, AI-powered trading ecosystem designed to transform how retail and professional traders operate, learn, and grow. It combines advanced analytics, real-time risk management, automated journaling, social trading, gamification, and AI coaching into a single unified platform.

Unlike traditional trading tools that focus solely on execution, Tradifive is built around the trader — their psychology, discipline, consistency, and long-term growth trajectory. The platform integrates directly with MetaTrader 5 (MT5) broker accounts to provide seamless data flow, automated trade tracking, and intelligent decision support.

Vision & Mission

"To become the operating system for every serious trader — where discipline meets technology, and growth is measurable."

Mission: To empower traders with intelligent tools that enforce discipline, automate journaling, manage risk in real-time, and build a supportive community — all within a single, beautifully designed platform.

Row Level Security (RLS)

All Supabase tables enforce RLS policies ensuring users can only SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, and DELETE their own data. Admin profiles have elevated privileges to bypass user-level restrictions.
